Validation & Structural Auditing Specs

  • Finite Element Analysis (FEA): Modeled in Karamba3D and validated under Titan-representative aerodynamic forces. Red zones indicate maximum bending stresses under high drag, showing a margin of safety exceeding 2.2 using anisotropic carbon-composite trusses.
  • Dual-Mode Kinematic Transitions: Dual-mode rovers shift from ground driving to rotor flight by sliding arm extensions outwards on linear guides, widening the rotor diameter to 1.5m to maximize lift and stability.
  • UE5 Physics-Based Digital Twin: Terrain, heights, and material friction indexes are generated from radar heightmap displacement grids. Custom rigid body controllers calibrate atmospheric density (`5.3 kg/m³`) and gravity (`1.35 m/s²`) to validate task plan latency.
  • Cooperative Consensus: Under latency lag, the 9 agents coordinate using distributed consensus voting. Task allocation, Lakeside soil LiDAR sweeps, and routing sequences are computed locally, preventing master-node bottlenecks.
