Computational design for autonomous multi-robot assembly.

The challenge of robot collectives delivering collaborative tasks in dynamic, unstructured construction environments sits exactly at the intersection my research has been converging on: integrated digital workflows that unify architectural design, structural and material logic, robotic-assembly constraints, and fabrication requirements within one computational framework.
